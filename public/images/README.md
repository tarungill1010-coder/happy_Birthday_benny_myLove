# Your photos

Temporary original SVG illustrations are included so every photo slot has artwork while you prepare your pictures. Replace them with your own photos, or update the paths in `src/data/birthdayContent.js`:

- `our-photo.svg` — the temporary home image; replace it with your one photo together
- `photo1.svg` through `photo5.svg` — temporary illustrations for her solo-photo timeline/gallery slots
- `final.svg` — the temporary finale illustration

To use your own files, for example `our-photo.jpg` or `photo1.jpg`, put them in this folder and change the corresponding `.svg` paths to `.jpg` in `src/data/birthdayContent.js`. Change the home-photo caption in `heroPhoto` and solo-photo captions in the `photos` list as you like.

The included artwork is locally created vector illustration—not stock photos or real people. JPG, PNG, and WebP all work when you replace it.
